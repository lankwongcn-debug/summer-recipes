Watermelon & Grilled Halloumi Salad $19

Ingredients

  • Watermelon 200g, 2cm cubes
  • Halloumi 80g, 1cm slices
  • Rocket, large handful (~30g)
  • ½ Lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • Red onion, thinly sliced
  • 6–8 mint leaves
  • 1 tbsp toasted pepitas or flaked almonds

Dressing (make a bottle)

  • 3 parts olive oil : 1 part lime juice, a little honey, salt & pepper

Method

  1. Dry-fry halloumi (no oil), medium-high, 1–2 min each side until golden
  2. Base of rocket, cucumber and onion; add watermelon
  3. Top with hot halloumi, mint and pepitas
  4. Dress and serve immediately
Prep: cut watermelon in the morning and keep chilled. Don't dress ahead (goes watery). Cook halloumi to order.

Greek Chicken Souvlaki Wrap $18 · Bowl $21

Marinade (per 1kg chicken thigh)

  • 4 tbsp olive oil, 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp paprika
  • 1½ tsp salt, black pepper
  • Marinate at least 2 hrs (overnight best)

Tzatziki (1 tub, keeps 3–4 days)

  • 500g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
  • 2 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Dried dill or mint, salt

Wrap

  • 120g chicken, 1 pita/wrap, 2 tbsp tzatziki, tomato, cucumber, red onion, lettuce (optional: a few chips inside)

Bowl

  • 150g chicken, rice or chips, Greek salad (tomato, cucumber, olives, feta), side of tzatziki

Method

  1. Grill or bake thighs at 200°C for 20–25 min; slice into strips
  2. Warm pita on the grill
  3. Tzatziki → chicken → salad → roll; wrap half in foil
Prep: cook chicken in one batch in the morning, reheat to order.

Lemongrass Chicken Vermicelli Bowl $19

Vietnamese Bún Gà Nướng Sả

Marinade (per 1kg chicken thigh)

  • 3 lemongrass stalks (white part, finely chopped)
  • 4 garlic cloves, 2 shallots
  • 3 tbsp fish sauce, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tbsp oil, black pepper
  • Marinate at least 1 hr

Nước Chấm dressing (keeps 1 week)

  • 1 cup warm water + 4 tbsp sugar (dissolve), 4 tbsp fish sauce, 3 tbsp lime juice, 2 garlic cloves, a little chilli

One bowl

  • 60g dry rice vermicelli – boil 3–4 min, rinse cold, drain well
  • 130g chicken
  • Shredded lettuce, cucumber, carrot, bean sprouts
  • Mint & coriander, fried shallots, crushed peanuts
  • Small pot of nước chấm on the side

Method

  1. Grill or bake chicken at 200°C ~20 min, slightly charred
  2. Noodles at the bottom → salad one side → chicken the other → peanuts, shallots, herbs
Prep: cook noodles in the morning, keep chilled (served cold). Quick-pickle carrot in sugar + vinegar.

Salmon Poke Bowl – Lunch Size $24

One bowl

  • 150g sushi rice
  • 80g sashimi-grade salmon, 2cm cubes
  • Edamame, ¼ avocado, cucumber, shredded carrot, red cabbage
  • Seaweed salad or pickled ginger
  • Sesame seeds, nori strips, spring onion

Poke sauce (make a bottle)

  • 4 tbsp soy, 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp honey, grated ginger, sesame

Spicy mayo

  • 4 parts mayonnaise : 1 part sriracha

Method

  1. Toss salmon in poke sauce to order – don't marinate ahead
  2. Rice → toppings in sections → salmon in the middle → spicy mayo, sesame
Cost: 80g salmon instead of ~130g saves about $3–4 per bowl.
